# Sales Performance & Customer Insights

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to understand revenue trends, product performance, geographical sales distribution, and customer purchasing behavior.

The goal is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, and interactive dashboard development.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Excel & Power Query:** Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation
- **Python:** Data analysis using Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and data visualization
- **DAX:** Measures, calculated columns, and time-intelligence analysis

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing values, duplicates, data types, and inconsistent records using Excel and Power Query.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Analyzed revenue trends, top-performing products, country-wise sales, and customer purchasing patterns using Python.
3. **Data Modeling:** Created a date table and established relationships in Power BI.
4. **DAX Calculations:** Developed measures for revenue, month-over-month growth, year-over-year growth, and customer segmentation.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Built an interactive dashboard with KPI cards, charts, and slicers.

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Overall Performance
- Total revenue generated was approximately **19.23 million**.
- Total quantity recorded was approximately **10.58 million units**.
- The dataset contained approximately **53.6K unique invoices** and **5.9K identified customers**.

### 2. Product Performance
- **REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER** ranked first among the top 10 products, generating approximately **327,345** in revenue.
- **DOTCOM POSTAGE** and **WHITE HANGING HEART T-LIGHT HOLDER** ranked second and third.
- **ROTATING SILVER ANGELS T-LIGHT HLDR** ranked tenth and was the lowest-revenue product within the top 10, generating approximately **73,746**.

### 3. Geographical Performance
- The **United Kingdom** contributed the highest revenue.
- EIRE and the Netherlands were also among the leading markets.

### 4. Customer Purchasing Behavior
- Approximately **75.41%** of identified customers were repeat customers.
- Approximately **24.59%** were one-time customers.

### 5. Revenue Trends
- Monthly revenue fluctuated over the analysis period.
- Month-over-month and year-over-year growth analysis helped identify changes in revenue performance.

### 6. Positive and Negative Transactions
- Positive transaction revenue was approximately **20.76 million**.
- Negative transaction revenue reduced the total by approximately **1.52 million**.
- This highlights the importance of examining cancellations, returns, and other negative transactions when evaluating net revenue.

## Business Recommendations

- Analyze the factors contributing to the strong performance of top-selling products.
- Explore opportunities to increase sales in markets outside the United Kingdom.
- Develop customer retention strategies to encourage repeat purchases.
- Investigate periods of declining revenue to identify possible seasonal patterns.
- Review negative transactions to better understand their impact on overall revenue.
